# Kicking Chickens Farming Sim

An augmented reality (AR) mobile farming simulation game where players raise chickens, manage coops, collect eggs, and build up their farm economy in physical space.

## Engine & Tools
- **Engine:** Unity 2021.3.3f1
- **Target Platform:** Mobile (Android / iOS)
- **AR Frameworks:** AR Foundation, Vuforia Engine

## Major Features & Mechanics
- **AR World Placement:** Uses surface plane detection and AR anchors to place the farm and coops directly into real-world environments.
- **Chicken & Coop Management:** Feed and tend to chickens, manage egg incubation and hatching, and interact with/kick chickens around the coop.
- **Farm Economy:** Collect eggs and sell farm produce to expand facilities, purchase feed, and upgrade coops.
- **Mobile UI & Touch Controls:** Touch gestures and animated mobile menus for interacting with chickens, objects, and store menus.
